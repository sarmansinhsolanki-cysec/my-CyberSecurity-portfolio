About

Sarmansinh Solanki, a security researcher working across HackerOne and Bugcrowd, focused on web application security — the layer where authentication, business logic, and trust boundaries actually break.

My process starts with recon: mapping the real surface before touching a single endpoint. From there it's tracing auth flows, chaining smaller findings into something that matters, and writing it up clearly enough that a triager doesn't have to guess.
